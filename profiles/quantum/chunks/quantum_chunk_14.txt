These are useful as one of the ways we can represent a state of a quantum computation is through a column vector. It turns out that when we multiply a matrix with a column vector, we get another column vector. Intuitively, what is happening is the column vector is getting transformed by the matrix, so we can use matrices as transformations on a vector. Here are some examples graphically.

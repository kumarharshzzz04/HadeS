A standard quantum function looks like this, where we input x and y and get back x and y exclusive or with f of x. Now you might be thinking, how are we going to get the output if it is exclusive or with y? But if we set y to zero, then we get zero exclusive or with f of x. Zero exclusive or with any bit gives us back that bit. This means that zero exclusive or with f of x is just f of x. This allows us to query the function and get its output on a quantum computer.

, we saw in the previous lessons that we could also rotate around the z axis. This is called phase. It may not seem very useful, as the probability of measuring a zero or a one is still the same, no matter how much we rotate around. But phase is what makes quantum computers as powerful as they are. We will get into the uses of phase in the next lesson. In this lesson, we will see how we can represent phase mathematically. To represent phase, we have to bring back our old friend, complex numbers. In quantum computing, we mostly use complex numbers in exponential form. You will now see why.

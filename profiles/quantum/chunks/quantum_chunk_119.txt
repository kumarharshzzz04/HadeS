The quantum phase estimation algorithm allows us to find the eigenvalue of an eigenvector given the matrix. So if we have a matrix u and apply it to its eigenvector v, since v is an eigenvector, we get e to the i theta v, where e to the i theta is the eigenvalue. We can represent all eigenvalues in quantum computing in this form. The QPE, or quantum phase estimation algorithm, finds e to the i theta to a certain accuracy.

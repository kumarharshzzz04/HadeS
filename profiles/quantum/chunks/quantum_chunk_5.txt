A standard complex number looks like this, a plus i b, where both a and b are real numbers, a being the real part of the number, and b being the imaginary part of the number. Complex numbers may not seem very useful, but in the next lesson, we will see how we can represent complex numbers in another form, which is used to model quantum computing states. We can add and subtract complex numbers pretty easily. All we do is add or subtract the real parts and the imaginary parts. Pause the video and try adding up these complex numbers.

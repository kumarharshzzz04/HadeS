We say a state is entangled if it cannot be factored into the tensor of single qubit states. For example, this state is not entangled since we can factor it like this, but this state is entangled since we cannot factor it into singular qubit states. What this means is that when the qubits are entangled, they depend on each other to determine their state. There are two types of entangled states, maximally entangled states and partially entangled states.

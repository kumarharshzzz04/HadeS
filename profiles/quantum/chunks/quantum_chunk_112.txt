Let's see how the number five is encoded once the quantum Fourier transform has been applied. As you can see, the right qubit has been rotated around the z-axis five over eight times two pi radians, which is five pi on four radians. To get the amount rotated around the z-axis of the next qubit, we multiply the previous one by two. So the next qubit has been rotated two times five over eight times two pi radians.

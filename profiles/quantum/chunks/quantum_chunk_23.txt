In terms of quantum computing, we say a qubit is in superposition, if it is both 0 and 1 at the same time. Let's look at how we represent qubits mathematically. Mathematically, we can represent a qubit as a column vector with two elements. The top element indicates how much the qubit is in the zero state, and the bottom element indicates how much the qubit is in the one state. The convention for a quantum state is to set it equal to the Greek letter psi. We'll talk more about this in the next video.

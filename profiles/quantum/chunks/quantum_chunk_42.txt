By multiplying the one state of the qubit with the complex number e to the i pi, we rotate the qubit around the z axis by pi radians. But why is it the one state and not the zero state being multiplied by the complex number? There are two types of phase, global phase and relative phase. Global phase is when the entire qubit is multiplied by a complex number. And relative phase is when just the one state is multiplied by a complex number. It turns out the global phase is physically irrelevant.

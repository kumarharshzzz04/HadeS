Just like classical computers, we still use zeros and ones, but in quantum computation, we define them instead as the column vectors 1, 0 and 0, 1 respectively. The weird brackets around 0 and 1 is Dirac notation. We will look more into it later. You may have heard of the term superposition. What it means in quantum mechanics is that a quantum particle is in two states at the same time. So back to our photon polarization example. This means that the photon is both horizontally and vertically polarized at the same time.

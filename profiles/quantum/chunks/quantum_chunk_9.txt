Using trigonometry, we find that we can also represent complex numbers in the form r cos theta plus i sin theta, where r is the magnitude of the complex number, and theta is the angle it makes with the positive x axis. We can find theta by using the tan function, as tan of theta is equal to b over a, so theta is arc tan of b over a. This is called polar form. There is also one other form, which is re to the power of i theta. This form is exponential form.

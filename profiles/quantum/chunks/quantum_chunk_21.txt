Quantum computers on the other hand use qubits or quantum bits, which can be 0 and 1 at the same time. We have all heard this explanation of quantum computers, but what does that actually mean? In order to understand what a quantum computer is actually doing, we must look at how we represent them mathematically. Instead of using binary, like classical computers, quantum computers use qubits. Physically, a qubit can be any quantum particle that exists in two distinct states, such as a photon of light being polarized either horizontally or vertically.

say we have the equation x squared is equal to 4, which means x is equal to plus or minus 2. Easy. But what if x squared is equal to negative 4? Since the number squared is always greater than 0, how can x squared ever be a negative number? This is where imaginary numbers come in. If we let i be the square root of negative 1, we can then write the square root of negative 4 as plus or minus 2i. This is an imaginary number, any number that contains a factor of the square root of negative 1, or i. Now we move one level up to complex numbers. All a complex number is, is a number that contains a real number, plus an imaginary number.

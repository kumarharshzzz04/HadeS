On the top, we have the 0 state, and on the bottom, we have the 1 state. On the horizontal, we have the plus state, minus state, i state, and negative i state. We will discuss these in later lessons. The closer up the qubit is to the 0 state, the higher probability of measuring a 0. And the same goes for the 1 state. That means that if we have the state 1 on root 2, 1 on root 2, it will lie halfway between the north and south poles, since it has an even chance of being measured as 0 or 1.

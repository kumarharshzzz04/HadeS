Ask your lectures.

HadeS turns a recorded course into something you can talk to. Every answer comes from what your teacher actually said — and points at the minute it happens.

What it is

A study companion for recorded lectures. It learns a course once, then answers questions from that course only, with the lesson and timestamp behind every reply.

Who it's for

Students sitting on hours of class recordings — and anyone who has tried to find one explanation inside a three-hour video.

Why it exists

Re-watching to locate a single moment spends the time the lecture already spent. HadeS gives the moment back in seconds, without sending your course anywhere.

How a question travels

  1. 1Ask anything

    Type the question the way you would ask a friend — no keywords, no lecture number.

  2. 2Find the exact lesson

    It reads across the whole course and picks the chapter that actually teaches this.

  3. 3Answer with the minute

    A plain-language answer, carrying the lesson and the time it came from.

  4. 4Play the moment

    The lecture opens inside the answer, already seeked to the second.
